# Memora Privacy Policy & Guarantees

Memora is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy.

## Privacy Guarantees

1. **Local-First Storage**
   All captured screen frames, text extractions, OCR data, accessibility trees, and local database entries remain strictly on your device in `~/.memora/`.

2. **No Analytics or Telemetry**
   Memora does not transmit usage tracking, clickstream telemetry, or session analytics to remote servers.

3. **Privacy Exclusion Rules**
   You can configure app ignore rules, window title filters, URL regex patterns, and private browsing (incognito) auto-suppression to prevent confidential material from ever being captured.

4. **PII Removal**
   Memora includes local PII redaction capabilities (secret detection, email/SSN masking) to sanitize text before storing or exposing to AI prompts.
